Project Page Paper Process Reward Policy-invariant Shaping

Research Framing

In robotic manipulation, a final success signal is often too sparse. Process reward modeling provides dense supervision by judging whether intermediate states move the robot closer to task completion.

Model Signals

  • Multi-view visual observations.
  • Language instructions and subgoal descriptions.
  • Step-level progress and failure modes.

System Role

  • Reward scoring for rollout analysis.
  • Shaping signals for policy optimization.
  • Evaluation of long-horizon robotic task progress.
