About

Research-driven engineering, grounded in real systems.

My recent work focuses on turning frontier model ideas into measurable systems: omni-modal pre-training and modality alignment, video search under open-world user memories, post-training for safety models, VLA-inspired long-horizon understanding, distillation pipelines for video and world models, and post-training pipelines for content safety.

I am especially interested in the intersection of multimodal foundation models, VLA / embodied intelligence, retrieval and recommendation, and efficient model training/inference systems. I enjoy problems where pre-training data, modality alignment, post-training signals, evaluation protocol, and engineering path must be designed together. I am currently an EECS undergraduate at BUPT.

Selected Research

Projects and manuscripts

Technical Report · Long Video Understanding

HY-Himmel Technical Report

A hierarchical long-video MLLM that pairs sparse semantic anchor I-frames with local interleaved RGB, motion-vector, and residual streams for dense temporal coverage at low token cost.

  • First author and Project Lead.
  • 63.5 on Video-MME, +2.3 points over the dense 32-frame baseline.
  • Uses 3.6× fewer context tokens through semantic-motion decomposition.
Project details Tech report
Ongoing Research · Process Reward Modeling

Robo-Dopamine 2.0

An OOD-aware temporal-memory extension for robotic process reward modeling. It adds compact history panels to static multi-view evidence; for OOD examples, it preserves the perturbed before/after views while reconstructing history from the corresponding successful source trajectory.

  • Ongoing research prototype; final results are not yet published.
  • Built on Robo-Dopamine by Tan et al., without claiming authorship of that paper.
Ongoing project
CVPR 2026 · Image Editing Evaluation

Omni IIE Bench

A human-curated benchmark for practical image-editing reliability, with separate tracks for single-turn cross-scale consistency and multi-turn coordination.

  • CVPR 2026 co-author.
  • 1,725 single-turn dialogues and 260 multi-turn groups.
  • Diagnoses regional fidelity, instruction compliance, and accumulated error.

Open-Domain Video Shot Retrieval

ShotFinder

An imagination-driven system that expands shot descriptions into video-level web queries, retrieves candidate videos, and localizes the target shot with adaptive multimodal grounding.

  • Second author with equal contribution.
  • 1,210 verified samples across 20 topics and six retrieval settings.

Benchmark · Real-World Video Memory Search

RVMS-Bench

A 1,440-sample benchmark for finding long videos and moments on the open web from fragmented global, key-moment, temporal, and auditory memories.

  • Third author with equal contribution.
  • 20 categories and four duration ranges up to 60 minutes.
