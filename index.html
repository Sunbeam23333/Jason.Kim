About

Research-driven engineering, grounded in real systems.

My recent work focuses on turning frontier model ideas into measurable systems: omni-modal pre-training and modality alignment, video search under open-world user memories, post-training for safety models, VLA-inspired long-horizon understanding, distillation pipelines for video and world models, and post-training pipelines for content safety.

I am especially interested in the intersection of multimodal foundation models, VLA / embodied intelligence, retrieval and recommendation, and efficient model training/inference systems. I enjoy problems where pre-training data, modality alignment, post-training signals, evaluation protocol, and engineering path must be designed together. I am currently an EECS undergraduate at BUPT.

Recommendation · Molecular Representation

FMASH: Multiscale Symptom-Herb Association Fusion

A traditional Chinese medicine formula recommendation framework that combines symptom-herb heterogeneous graphs, molecular-level herb representations, missing feature completion, and ordered prescription generation.

  • Uses GCN for local graph structure and BiMamba for long-range node dependency.
  • Integrates UniMol molecular embeddings with herb attributes through attention and gating.
  • Improves top-K precision, recall, and F1 over prior baselines on TCM-PD.
