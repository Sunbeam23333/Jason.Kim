EVID-Bench

When Seeing Is Not Believing—A Benchmark for Search-Grounded Video Misinformation Detection

ACL Anthology Code Dataset
My role

Co-author with Tao Yu, Yujia Yang, Shenghua Chai, Jinshuai Zhang and colleagues.

Finding the footage that changes the interpretation

An edit can reverse the apparent order of two events while every frame still looks authentic. EVID-Bench asks a model to find related footage on the open web, compare the accounts, and explain which details in the input video are misleading.

What the evaluation found

The benchmark includes 222 videos and nine types of manipulation drawn from three categories: AI generation, edits to a single source, and edits that combine sources. Across nine multimodal models, the best system scored 61.43% on individual points of misinformation and 43.24% at the video level. Models often found a relevant clue and stopped searching before they could explain the full edit.
