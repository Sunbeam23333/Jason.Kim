About

Research-driven engineering, grounded in real systems.

My recent work focuses on turning frontier model ideas into measurable systems: video search that works under open-world user memories, distillation pipelines for video and world models, multimodal embedding pre-training, and post-training pipelines for content safety.

I am especially interested in the intersection of multimodal foundation models, retrieval and recommendation, and efficient model training. I enjoy problems where the research question, data construction, evaluation protocol, and engineering path must be designed together. I am currently an EECS undergraduate at BUPT.

Selected Research

Projects and manuscripts

Recommendation · Molecular Representation

FMASH: Multiscale Symptom-Herb Association Fusion

A traditional Chinese medicine formula recommendation framework that combines symptom-herb heterogeneous graphs, molecular-level herb representations, missing feature completion, and ordered prescription generation.

  • Uses GCN for local graph structure and BiMamba for long-range node dependency.
  • Integrates UniMol molecular embeddings with herb attributes through attention and gating.
  • Improves top-K precision, recall, and F1 over prior baselines on TCM-PD.

Distillation Systems

WorldDistill

A unified distillation framework for video generation and world models, covering model cataloging, teacher-student runtime, cache-aware supervision, few-step distillation, streaming, consistency, and context forcing.

Full-modality embedding pre-training built on text, image, video, audio, ASR, OCR, and video summary signals, connecting omni-modal representation learning to retrieval, reranking, and recommendation systems.
